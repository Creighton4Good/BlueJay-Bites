How it works

Three roles, one loop from surplus to shared.

BlueJay-Bites is built around the people who make sharing surplus food actually happen on campus.

🔎
Users

Find food nearby

Browse active food events across campus on a live feed and map, see what's available and where, and head over before it's gone.

📣
Event organizers

Post the surplus

When an event ends with extra food, organizers post it in seconds — location, food type, and how long it's available — and manage their own listings.

🛡️
Admins

Keep it running

Admins oversee events across campus, manage access and roles, and keep the platform healthy and trustworthy for the whole community.

Why we built it

BlueJay-Bites started with a simple mismatch: campus events throw out good food while students go hungry. We wanted to close that gap with something people would actually reach for.

The app is a project of Creighton4Good, a faculty-guided, student-led open-source lab. It's built in the spirit of Creighton's Jesuit mission — putting technical work in service of the community around it.

Open source, and meant to travel

Food waste and food insecurity aren't unique to one campus. BlueJay-Bites is designed to become a replicable framework other universities can adopt and adapt.

  • Student-built and openly developed on GitHub
  • Configurable for a different campus, not hard-wired to ours
  • Documented so the next team can pick it up
